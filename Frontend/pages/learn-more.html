Learn More About AI-Based Smart Safety System

Understand how our AI-powered solution prevents industrial accidents.

🎯 Problem Statement

Industrial environments involve heavy machinery where human presence can lead to severe accidents. Traditional safety systems fail to detect real-time human-machine interaction.

💡 Our Solution

The AI-Based Smart Safety System uses computer vision and real-time monitoring to detect humans near machines and instantly trigger alerts or stop operations to prevent accidents.

⚙️ How the System Works

  1. Camera captures live video near machines
  2. AI model detects human presence
  3. Danger zones are analyzed
  4. Alerts are generated in milliseconds
  5. Machine is stopped if risk is detected

🧠 Technologies Used

  • YOLO (You Only Look Once) – Human Detection
  • Computer Vision
  • Python & AI Models
  • Real-Time Dashboard
  • Web Technologies (HTML, CSS, JS)

✅ Key Benefits

  • Prevents industrial accidents
  • Real-time monitoring
  • Fast response time
  • Improves worker safety
  • Reduces machine damage

🏭 Use Cases

Suitable for factories, workshops, construction sites, manufacturing units, and high-risk industrial zones.
