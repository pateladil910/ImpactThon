01. Scope & Safety-First Mandate

The AI Smart Safety Shield is an advanced, computer-vision gated proximity lockout system. It is designed to act as a secondary, hybrid millisecond-level lockout guard for industrial machinery. By accessing or deploying the Shield, all parties agree to the following terms:

  • No Substitution: This system is a safety assistance aid and must not be used as a replacement for standard physical barriers, optical light curtains, or mechanical interlocks required by OSHA or local industrial regulations.
  • No Bypass: Operators and administrative personnel are strictly prohibited from bypassing physical shields or disabling mechanical kill-switches under the assumption that the AI Shield will capture the intrusion.
  • Visual Verification: Operators must always follow standard hand-on-lock physical lockout tagout (LOTO) protocols before placing limbs or body parts into hazardous machine zones.
🚨 Critical Industrial Directive

Never rely solely on software-based detection for protection against life-threatening hazards. The AI Smart Safety Shield operates alongside physical barriers, never in place of them.

02. Role Clearance & System Override Authority

The system segregates user access based on role tiers handled securely by Google Firebase Authentication. You agree to respect the limits of your assigned role tier:

  • Viewer clearance: Read-only access to home dashboards and telemetry analytics charts. Viewers have zero authorization to alter settings, stream configs, or input diagnostic override flags.
  • Operator clearance: Granted access to deploy camera nodes, configure warning zones, and stream active feeds. Operators are responsible for verifying camera angles and zone calibration accuracy.
  • Admin clearance: Granted full administrative permissions, including system overrides, hardware release lockouts, and calibration parameters. Admins hold full responsibility for any software override commands issued to active relay circuits.
⚠️ Administrative Liability Notice

Issuing an override release command bypasses the active AI lockout trigger. Admins must inspect the physical plant floor visually before issuing overrides.

03. Telemetry Stream & Hardware Network Duty

Real-time machine lockout triggers require high-fidelity streams and fail-safe relay operation. Compliance with the following technical boundaries is mandatory:

  • Frame Rate & Resolution: Streams must be maintained at a minimum resolution of 640x480 pixels and an active rate of 30 frames per second (FPS). Reduced framerates directly delay zone violation reaction times.
  • Network Latency: Local network latency between the Edge Camera setups, backend Node.js APIs, and Flask AI microservice must not exceed 100ms. If packet drop rates exceed 5%, the system will automatically assume danger state.
  • Relay Fail-Safe States: The ESP32 relay shield must be wired in a fail-safe configuration where power loss or serial disconnect defaults the contactors to an open (de-energized) machine stop state.

04. Data Acquisition, Logs & Alert Broadcasts

To audit incidents and secure hazardous zones, the safety shield gathers telemetry and transmits incident alerts:

  • Violation Snapshots: Upon detecting zone violations (Danger state), the Flask AI service automatically packages the violating frame as an encrypted base64 payload to log in the database.
  • SMTP Alert Gateways: Safe zone breaches dispatch high-priority email alerts including frame snapshots directly to operator control centers via Mailcloud SMTP relays.
  • Security Verification: Telemetry data including machine metadata, zone coordinate parameters, operator identities, and hardware serial codes are stored securely using AES-256-CBC encryption algorithms.

05. Limitation of Liability

The AI models (YOLOv8) utilize probabilistic algorithms to track human coordinates. Environmental factors (dust, lens smudges, smoke, poor lighting, safety clothing patterns) may affect classification accuracy. Code Vortex Technologies Inc. provides this system under the following liability releases:

  • Probabilistic Nature: Under no circumstances does Code Vortex warrant that the system will detect 100% of hazard conditions under all circumstances.
  • Force Majeure / Network Failure: Code Vortex is not liable for accidents resulting from network interruptions, cloud hosting warmup latencies (such as Render spin-downs), or electrical surges affecting ESP32 chips.
  • Damages Caps: In no event shall Code Vortex Technologies Inc. be liable for any direct, indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from industrial accidents, mechanical halts, or equipment delays.
